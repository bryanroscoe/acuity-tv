The method · consistent for every title

Three lenses.
One honest number.

Every score reads how much a title gives back across three lenses, then resolves to a single number on a fixed 0–200 scale. The same three lenses, applied to every title — no editorial gospel, no studio thumb on the scale.

DepthPrimary

How much the work asks of your attention — ideas, ambiguity, structure, the thinking it demands while you watch.

InsightSecondary

What you carry out of it — knowledge, perspective, a way of seeing the world you didn't have going in.

CraftSupporting

How well it's made — writing, performance, image and sound working in service of the idea, not decorating it.

Calibrated, not published The three lenses are weighted and combined into one number, tuned against thousands of titles. We keep the exact weighting in-house — a score you can trust shouldn't be a one-line equation anyone can reverse-engineer or game.
The distribution

We grade on a flat curve.

Most rating sites pile every title into a tall spike at the middle and call it a bell. Acuity spreads titles honestly across the whole range — a deliberately flat curve with real weight in the tails, so the Profound few and the Idle many are both easy to find.
